SOC Code: s47-1011.00
First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers

About the Role
Directly supervise and coordinate activities of construction or extraction workers.
What You'll Do
Inspect work progress, equipment, or construction sites to verify safety or to ensure that specifications are met.
Read specifications, such as blueprints, to determine construction requirements or to plan procedures.
Supervise, coordinate, or schedule the activities of construction or extractive workers.
Assign work to employees, based on material or worker requirements of specific jobs.
Coordinate work activities with other construction project activities.
Estimate material or worker requirements to complete jobs.
Analyze worker or production problems and recommend solutions.
Order or requisition materials or supplies.
Train workers in construction methods, operation of equipment, safety procedures, or company policies.
Locate, measure, and mark site locations or placement of structures or equipment.

Tools & Technology
Microsoft Project (Project management software)
Microsoft Excel (Spreadsheet software)
Adobe Acrobat (Document management software)
Education & Experience
Education: High school diploma or equivalent or Post-secondary certificate
Related Work Experience: Over 10 years
Work Environment
Frequent face-to-face discussions
Wear safety equipment
Responsible for health and safety of workers
High frequency of decision making

SOC Code: s47-2011.00
Boilermakers

About the Role
Construct, assemble, maintain, and repair stationary steam boilers and boiler house auxiliaries. Align structures or plate sections to assemble boiler frame tanks or vats, following blueprints. Work involves use of hand and power tools, plumb bobs, levels, wedges, dogs, or turnbuckles.
What You'll Do
Conduct pressure tests on vessels, such as boilers.
Study blueprints to determine locations, relationships, or dimensions of parts.
Examine boilers, pressure vessels, tanks, or vats to locate defects such as leaks or weak spots.
Inspect assembled vessels or individual components to locate any defects.
Lay out plate, sheet steel, or other heavy metal and locate and mark bending and cutting lines.
Bell, bead with power hammers, or weld pressure vessel tube ends to ensure leakproof joints.
Shape or fabricate parts such as stacks, uptakes, or chutes to adapt pressure vessels.
Clean pressure vessel equipment using scrapers, wire brushes, and cleaning solvents.

Tools & Technology
Autodesk AutoCAD (CAD software)
Microsoft Excel (Spreadsheet software)
SAP software (ERP software)
Education & Experience
Education: High school diploma or equivalent or Post-secondary certificate
On-the-Job Training: 2 to 4 years
Related Work Experience: 4 years to 6 years
Work Environment
Wear protective safety equipment
Exposed to very hot or cold temperatures
Importance of being exact or accurate


SOC Code: j47-2051.00
Cement Masons and Concrete Finishers

About the Role
Smooth and finish surfaces of poured concrete, such as floors, walks, sidewalks, roads, or curbs using a variety of hand and power tools. Align forms for sidewalks, curbs, or gutters; patch voids; and use saws to cut expansion joints.
What You'll Do
Check the forms that hold the concrete to see that they are properly constructed.
Set forms to the desired pitch and depth, and align them.
Spread, level, and smooth concrete using rake, shovel, hand or power trowel, and float.
Monitor how wind, heat, or cold affect the curing of the concrete throughout the process.
Mold expansion joints and edges using edging tools, jointers, and straightedge.
Direct the casting of the concrete and supervise laborers who spread it.
Apply hardening and sealing compounds to cure and waterproof the surface.
Operate power vibrator to compact concrete.

Tools & Technology
Estimating software (Project management software)
Concrete mix calculation software (Analytical software)
Education & Experience
Education: Less than high school diploma or High school diploma or equivalent
On-the-Job Training: Up to 1 month
Work Environment
Outdoors, exposed to all weather conditions
Wear safety equipment
Exposed to contaminants


SOC Code: j47-2061.00
Construction Laborers

About the Role
Perform tasks involving physical labor at construction sites. May operate hand and power tools of all types: air hammers, earth tampers, cement mixers, small mechanical hoists, surveying and measuring equipment, and a variety of other equipment and instruments. May clean and prepare sites, dig trenches, set braces to support excavations, erect scaffolding, and clean up rubble, debris, and other waste materials.
What You'll Do
Tend pumps, compressors, or generators to provide power for tools or machinery.
Lubricate, clean, or repair machinery, equipment, or tools.
Signal equipment operators to facilitate alignment, movement, or adjustment of machinery.
Read plans, instructions, or specifications to determine work activities.
Measure, mark, or record openings or distances to lay out construction areas.
Clean or prepare construction sites to eliminate possible hazards.
Dig ditches or trenches, backfill excavations, or compact and level earth to grade.
Load, unload, or identify building materials, machinery, or tools.
Position, join, align, or seal structural components such as concrete wall sections or pipes.

Tools & Technology
Autodesk Revit (CAD software)
Microsoft Excel (Spreadsheet software)
Oracle Primavera (Project management software)
Education & Experience
Education: High school diploma or equivalent or Less than high school diploma
On-the-Job Training: 3 to 6 months
Work Environment
Outdoors, exposed to all weather conditions
Wear safety equipment
Exposed to hazardous equipment
Exposed to contaminants


SOC Code: j47-2131.00
Insulation Workers, Floor, Ceiling, and Wall

About the Role
Line and cover structures with insulating materials. May work with batt, roll, or blown insulation materials.
What You'll Do
Measure and cut insulation for covering surfaces, using tape measures, handsaws, power saws, knives, or scissors.
Fit, wrap, staple, or glue insulating materials to structures or surfaces, using hand tools or wires.
Cover and line structures with blown or rolled insulation materials to insulate against cold, heat, or moisture.
Distribute insulating materials evenly into small spaces within floors, ceilings, or walls.
Fill blower hoppers with insulating materials and regulate flow through nozzles.
Cover, seal, or finish insulated surfaces with plastic covers, canvas strips, sealants, or tape.
Read blueprints and select appropriate insulation based on space characteristics.
Remove old insulation, such as asbestos, following safety procedures.

Tools & Technology
Microsoft Excel (Spreadsheet software)
NAIMA 3E Plus insulation software (Analytical software)
Education & Experience
Education: High school diploma or equivalent or Less than high school diploma
On-the-Job Training: Up to 1 month
Work Environment
Wear specialized protective equipment including breathing apparatus
Exposed to contaminants
Exposed to very hot or cold temperatures
